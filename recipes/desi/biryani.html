Biryani

Description

Biryani is a flavorful South Asian rice dish made by layering fragrant basmati rice with marinated meat or vegetables, aromatic spices, and herbs. It is slow-cooked so that the rice absorbs the rich flavors, resulting in a delicious and satisfying meal. Biryani is often served with raita (yogurt sauce), salad, or a spicy curry.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 500 g chicken (or mutton)
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste
  • 2-3 green chilies
  • ½ cup fresh coriander leaves, chopped
  • ½ cup fresh mint leaves
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 4 cloves
  • 2 cardamom pods
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • Salt to taste
  • 4 tablespoons cooking oil or ghee
  • Saffron soaked in warm milk (optional)

Steps to Make Biryani

  1. Wash the basmati rice and soak it in water for 30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil or ghee in a large pot and fry the sliced onions until golden brown. Set half of them aside for garnish.
  3. Add the whole spices (bay leaves, cloves, cardamom, cinnamon, and cumin seeds) and cook for about 30 seconds.
  4. Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook until fragrant.
  5. Add the chicken, yogurt, tomatoes, turmeric, red chili powder, garam masala, and salt. Cook until the chicken is tender.
  6. Stir in the chopped mint and coriander leaves.
  7. In another pot, boil the soaked rice until it is about 70% cooked, then drain.
  8. Layer the partially cooked rice over the chicken mixture.
  9. Sprinkle the reserved fried onions and saffron milk (if using) over the rice.
  10. Cover the pot tightly and cook on low heat for 20-25 minutes.
  11. Gently mix the layers before serving.
  12. Serve hot with raita, salad, or pickle.
