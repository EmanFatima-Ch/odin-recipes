Margherita Pizza

Description

Margherita Pizza is a classic Italian pizza known for its simple and fresh ingredients. It is made with a thin, crispy crust topped with tomato sauce, mozzarella cheese, and fresh basil leaves. The combination of red tomatoes, white cheese, and green basil represents the colors of the Italian flag and creates a delicious balance of flavors.

Ingredients

For Pizza Dough
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon instant yeast
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¾ cup warm water
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
For Topping
  • ½ cup pizza sauce or crushed tomatoes
  • 200 grams fresh mozzarella cheese, sliced
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • 1-2 tablespoons olive oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper (optional)

Steps to Follow

  1. In a bowl, mix warm water, sugar, and yeast. Let it sit for 5-10 minutes until it becomes foamy.
  2. Add flour, salt, and olive oil. Knead the mixture into a soft dough.
  3. Cover the dough and let it rest for 1-2 hours until it doubles in size.
  4. Preheat the oven to 220°C (425°F).
  5. Roll out the dough into a thin round shape and place it on a baking tray.
  6. Spread pizza sauce evenly over the dough.
  7. Add slices of mozzarella cheese and sprinkle with a little salt and pepper.
  8. Bake the pizza for 10-15 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted.
  9. Remove the pizza from the oven and add fresh basil leaves on top.
  10. Drizzle with olive oil, slice, and serve hot.
