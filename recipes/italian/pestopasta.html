Pesto Pasta

Description

Pesto Pasta is a simple and flavorful Italian dish made by tossing cooked pasta with fresh basil pesto sauce. The pesto is prepared using basil leaves, Parmesan cheese, garlic, pine nuts, olive oil, and seasonings, giving the pasta a rich, herby, and aromatic taste. It is often served as a main course and can be enjoyed with grilled chicken, vegetables, or garlic bread.

Ingredients

  • 250 grams pasta (penne, spaghetti, or fusilli)
  • 2 cups fresh basil leaves
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • ¼ cup pine nuts (or walnuts)
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • ⅓ cup extra virgin olive oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper to taste
  • Cherry tomatoes, halved (optional)
  • Fresh basil leaves for garnish

Steps to Follow

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the pasta according to the package instructions until al dente. Drain and reserve a small amount of the pasta water.
  2. In a food processor or blender, combine the fresh basil leaves, garlic, pine nuts, Parmesan cheese, olive oil, salt, and black pepper. Blend until smooth to make the pesto sauce.
  3. Place the cooked pasta in a large bowl or pan and add the prepared pesto sauce.
  4. Toss the pasta until it is evenly coated. Add a small amount of the reserved pasta water if needed to loosen the sauce.
  5. If desired, stir in the halved cherry tomatoes for extra freshness and flavor.
  6. Taste and adjust the seasoning with additional salt and black pepper if needed.
  7. Garnish with extra Parmesan cheese and fresh basil leaves.
  8. Serve immediately while warm with garlic bread or a fresh salad.
